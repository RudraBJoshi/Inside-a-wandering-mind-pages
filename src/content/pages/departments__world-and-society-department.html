World & Society Department

Explore how our culture, human history, and global shifts impact us today. Together, we will better understand the complexities of our shared world to uncover stories and truths that matter.

An Introduction

Hi! I’m Sophia, and I lead the World and Society Department at IAWM. I believe that our human connection and our spark is what brings writing to life! I also love cats and food, so if you relate maybe you’ve found yourself in the right place. I hope you all find writing as enjoyable as I do, and find yourself inspired by the writing we will be curating as a department. My goal for IAWM is to create a space for people to share their opinions and beliefs in an informative way that can be used to change how we see the world. Thank you for visiting our department page, and I hope for great things to come!

Signed, Sophia Xi

What is World and Society?

Explore our Writing

World and Society sounds very broad. If you think about it, this department could entail anything! However, the main focus of our writing is split up into these few subcategories: culture, economics, government, history, philosophy, society, and travel. If you find yourself interested in any of these, please go ahead and read whatever interests you. Even better, you can sign up to write your own pieces. World and Society explores the ideas that shape human life, ideas that are crucial to our critical thinking skills. We hope to encourage this philosophy of a deeper understanding of our world beyond just our physical surroudnings!

As mentioned in the box to the left, the writing is split up into 7 subcategories. Hopefully, more writing beyond Codys extensive travel posts will be posted here in the near future! But hopefully, you’ll find political or cultural commentary, philosophical analysis, or a historical deep dive. If that doesn’t interest you, maybe you could write something of your own!
